Colour Scheme

Primary Color

Purple was chosen as the main color, reflecting the brand identity and tying closely to the logo.

Background

A white background keeps the interface clean, clear, and easy to read, providing contrast against the primary and accent colors.

Design Rationale

This colour palette balances brand recognition with usability, creating a visually cohesive and engaging experience for users.

Typography

Modern and Clean

Montserrat was chosen for its clean, modern, and versatile design.

Readability & Hierarchy

Its geometric letterforms ensure clarity, making both interface elements and longer texts easy to read.

Design Rationale

The typeface balances professionalism and approachability, supporting a clear and user-friendly experience.
