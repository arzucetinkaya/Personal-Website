Design Approach

Colors

Visual Direction

A dark theme creates a focused, studio-like environment that supports creativity. Vibrant gradients highlight primary features, making core actions clear and adding energy to the interface.

Highlight

Pro subscription areas are accented in orange, guiding attention without disrupting the overall balance.

Design Trends

These colour choices reflect current AI design trends. Dark backgrounds, bold gradients, and bright accents that convey innovation and clarity.

Typography

Modern & Clean

Capton was selected for its modern, clean, and versatile design. The typeface reinforces the innovative, user-focused nature of the application, contributing to a clear and cohesive visual identity.

Readability & Hierarchy

Its geometric yet friendly letterforms provide clarity and approachability. Capton balances professionalism with readability, making it suitable for interface elements as well as longer instructional texts.

Outcomes

User Acquisition

Subscription Rates

Research was conducted on paywalls, leading to the design of six different variations. Two paywalls were selected for A/B testing. Based on the results, the final design was chosen for implementation. After launch, the new paywall increased subscription rates by nearly 50%.

Churned Users

Pop-up designs and dedicated screens were created to re-engage churned users and encourage them to resubscribe. These designs contributed to a reduction in churn, helping retain more subscribers and strengthen overall subscription growth.

+1 Million Users

The app reached over a million users and stayed top 1 in its category for months in countries such as USA, UK, Canada, New Zealand and more.
